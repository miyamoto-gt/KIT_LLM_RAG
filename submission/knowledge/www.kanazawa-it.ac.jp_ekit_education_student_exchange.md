## Education & Research
### Student exchange and overseas training
##### Long-term student exchange program
KIT conducts a long-term student exchange program with the partner institutions in the US.
-
Program for undergraduate students
Place of Study : Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology or Rochester Institute of Technology
Period of Study : 4 to 6 months
-
Program for graduate students
Place of Study : University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign, Rochester Institute of Technology, or University of Hawaii at Manoa
Period of Study : about 9 months
##### Short-term overseas programs
KIT conducts four short-term overseas programs during a holiday period in Summer and Spring.
-
English Training Program in New Zealand
Place of Study : Unitec Institute of Technology
Period of Study : 4 weeks in March
-
English Training Program in United Kingdom
Place of Study : University of Leicester
Period of Study : 3 weeks in August
-
KIT/KTC Learning Express
Place of Activity : Indonesia, Thailand, or Vietnam
Period of Study : about 2 weeks each in September and March
-
Summer Program in Thailand
Place of Study : Thai-Nichi Institute of Technology
Period of Study : about 10 days from late August to early September
