KIT JLP Summer 2026
Hello and welcome to KIT Japanese Language Program (JLP) Summer! This program is offered as part of the student exchange agreements between KIT and its sister schools. It aims to give them rich experiences in 1) improving communication skills in Japanese language and 2) understanding Japanese culture and society through lectures and cultural programs.
