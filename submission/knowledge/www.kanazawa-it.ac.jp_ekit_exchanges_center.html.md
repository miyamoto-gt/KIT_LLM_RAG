## International Exchanges
### Center for International Programs
#### Mission and Service
##### Mission
International Programs is the Vanguard of Global Education
As the world is changing rapidly and national borders are becoming blurred, interaction between peoples of different countries is more common and increasingly important. Because one of our missions is to produce graduates who can contribute to the international community, KIT actively promotes global education through various international activities.
##### Services
- Administration of KIT Japanese Language Program (KIT JLP)
- Advising KIT students in study abroad programs
- Administration of KIT Exchange Programs and the other Study Abroad Programs
